Custom LED track lighting is how a brand stops selling "commodity lights" and starts selling a product only it offers. But "custom" means very different things at different levels — from a quick logo swap to a fully re-engineered fixture — and each level has its own **MOQ and lead time**. This guide breaks down exactly what you can customize, what it costs in time and volume, and how to plan an order that actually ships.

If you are not sure whether you need OEM or ODM yet, our [custom track lighting manufacturer guide](/how-to-choose-custom-track-lighting-manufacturer-2026/) explains the sourcing models first.

## The 5 Levels of Customization

Before you talk MOQ, decide how deep you want to go. Custom LED track lighting spans five levels, each with different tooling and minimums:

1. **Branding only** — logo on a standard [VEGA](/products/track-lighting/), custom packaging. Lowest MOQ, fastest.
2. **Configuration** — pick from existing wattage, CCT, CRI, beam, and dimming options. No new tooling.
3. **Cosmetic customization** — custom housing color/finish, reflector, lens. Some tooling, moderate MOQ.
4. **Parameter re-engineering** — custom CCT binning, SDCM ≤ 3, custom optics or driver selection. Engineering involvement.
5. **Bespoke tooling** — a new housing or track profile designed for you. Highest MOQ and longest lead time.

## What You Can Customize: The Full Menu

### Housing color & finish

- **Colors** — any RAL/Pantone: black, white, silver, matte, anodized, or custom brand colors.
- **Finishes** — powder coat, anodizing, electroplating, textured, sandblasted.
- **Materials** — die-cast aluminum (standard), extruded aluminum, or custom profiles for magnetic track.

### Color temperature (CCT)

Choose standard bins or ask for a custom CCT in between:

- 2700K (warm hospitality), 3000K (retail/hotel), 3500K (mixed), 4000K (retail/commercial), 5000K–6500K (supermarket/daylight).
- Custom **SDCM ≤ 3** binning for color consistency across a project — critical for retail chains where fixtures sit side by side.

### Color rendering (CRI)

- Standard: CRI 80–90.
- Premium: **CRI 90+, CRI 95+** with strong R9 — the retail and museum baseline in 2026.
- Optional **UV/IR filtering** for artwork and merchandise protection.

### Beam angle & optics

- Narrow spot (15°–24°) for accent and display.
- Medium flood (30°–40°) for general retail.
- Wide flood / wall-wash (45°–60° and asymmetric) for grazing and ambient.
- **Custom reflector + lens engineering** with an IES file — the sign of a true factory, not a re-labeler.

### Wattage & electrical

- Typical range for track heads: 5W–50W per head.
- Custom driver selection for **constant current (CC)** or **constant voltage (CV)** platforms.
- Voltage options: 120V, 220–240V, 277V, or low-voltage (24V) magnetic track systems.

### Dimming protocols

- **TRIAC** — standard for North American residential dimmers.
- **0–10V** — commercial and architectural standard.
- **DALI / DALI-2** — building-management integration.
- **PWM / Zigbee / Casambi / Matter** — smart-home and wireless control for e-commerce brands.

### Branding & packaging

- Custom **logo printing** on the housing (laser engraving, silk screen, pad printing).
- **Retail-ready box** with your brand, EAN/UPC barcode, spec sticker, and inserts.
- **Custom cartons** with foam inserts, multilingual manuals, and compliance labels.
- Packaging MOQ is usually **lower** than the product MOQ — so you can test a market with a small run before scaling.

## MOQ by Customization Level

Here is what a realistic OEM/ODM factory will tell you about minimums:

| Customization level           | Typical MOQ             | Notes                                             |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Branding / packaging only     | 100–300 units           | Lowest barrier — good for market testing          |
| Configuration from catalog    | 300–500 units per model | No new tooling                                    |
| Cosmetic (color/finish)       | 500 units               | Reflector/lens change may add a small tooling fee |
| Parameter re-engineering      | 500–1,000 units         | Engineering + IES work                            |
| Bespoke tooling (new housing) | 1,000+ units            | Tooling cost amortized across the order           |

The headline number to remember: **around 500 units per model** is the standard OEM MOQ for custom LED track lighting, with flexible trial options and packaging MOQs that go lower.

## Realistic Lead Times

Lead time depends on how much you are customizing. Plan for these ranges:

- **Sample** — 7–15 days for a standard or lightly customized unit, plus courier.
- **Cosmetic / configuration order** — 20–35 days for bulk production.
- **Re-engineered order** — 30–45 days including prototype revision.
- **Bespoke tooling** — add 3–6 weeks for tooling and certification before production; a full first cycle is realistically **8–14 weeks**.

For repeat orders after the first run, lead times usually compress to **4–6 weeks** because tooling is already done. Get the schedule in writing, with production milestones, before you pay a deposit.

## Planning Your Custom Track Lighting Order

A smooth custom order follows a sequence:

1. **Write the spec** — application, photometrics, electrical, mechanical, compliance market. Use our [beam angle calculator](/beam-angle-calculator/) and [lux calculator](/tools/lux-calculator/) to lock numbers.
2. **Request the RFQ** — tell the factory which customization level you need so they quote MOQ and tooling correctly.
3. **Review the engineered sample + IES file** — verify beam, CCT, CRI, and SDCM against your spec.
4. **Approve and lock the spec** — after this, changes cost money.
5. **Production with QC** — IQC → IPQC → 100% aging → OQC.
6. **Shipment with documentation** — certificates naming your SKU ship with the goods.

## FAQ

**What is the minimum order quantity for custom LED track lighting?**
Around 500 units per model for genuine customization, with lower MOQs for branding/packaging-only orders (100–300) and higher for bespoke tooling (1,000+). Packaging MOQ is usually lower than product MOQ.

**What can I customize on LED track lighting?**
Housing color and finish, CCT (2700K–6500K with SDCM ≤ 3), CRI (80/90/95+ with R9 and UV/IR filtering), beam angle and custom optics (with IES file), wattage, dimming protocol (TRIAC, 0-10V, DALI, wireless), plus logo and packaging.

**How long does a custom track light order take?**
Samples in 7–15 days; cosmetic/config orders in 20–35 days; re-engineered orders in 30–45 days; bespoke tooling adds 3–6 weeks, for a full first cycle of 8–14 weeks. Repeat orders run 4–6 weeks.

**How deep should my customization be?**
Start with branding + configuration to test a market, then scale to cosmetic or re-engineered customization as your volume grows. Match the customization level to your order size and margin goals.

**Do I need an IES file with my custom fixture?**
Yes — an IES/LDT file lets you verify beam distribution in your design software before committing to production. Any serious factory provides one with your engineered prototype.

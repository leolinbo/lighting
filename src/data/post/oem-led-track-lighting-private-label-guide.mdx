## What Is OEM LED Track Lighting?

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) means a manufacturer produces a track lighting product **to your specification**, and you sell it under **your brand**. The product is yours to market; the factory handles the engineering, production, quality control, and compliance.

There are three common sourcing models — knowing which one you are asking for saves weeks of back-and-forth:

| Model             | What you own                           | Best for                                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **OEM**           | Your specs, your brand, your packaging | Brands that know exactly what they want to sell |
| **ODM**           | A modified catalog design, your brand  | Brands that want a proven design adapted fast   |
| **Private label** | A standard product, your logo on it    | Resellers launching a brand at minimum cost     |

Most "OEM LED track lighting" searches are really asking for **private label track lights** — your brand on a well-engineered, certifiable product with a few meaningful customizations. That is exactly what this guide covers.

## The 6-Step OEM Track Lighting Process

A reliable OEM track light project follows a defined sequence. Skip steps and you inherit the risk; follow them and you get a product that is buildable, certifiable, and on time.

### 1. Requirement communication (需求沟通)

Everything starts with a clear spec sheet. The more concrete you are here, the fewer costly surprises later. At minimum, send the factory:

- **Application** — retail, museum, hospitality, residential, office
- **Photometrics** — beam angle, lumens, CCT, CRI, target lux
- **Electrical** — voltage, wattage, dimming protocol
- **Mechanical** — track type (3-wire, 1-circuit, magnetic), housing style, finish, dimensions
- **Compliance market** — North America (ETL/UL), EU (CE), Australia (SAA), etc.

If you are unsure what your spec numbers should be, use our [color temperature guide](/tools/cct-guide) to pick CCT and our [CRI guide](/tools/cri-guide) to set a color-rendering target before you write the RFQ.

### 2. Prototyping (打样)

The factory builds an engineered prototype — not a catalog sample with your logo pasted on. A serious OEM prototype comes with:

- A **photometric (IES/LDT) file** so you can verify beam angle and distribution in your own lighting design software
- Measured **beam angle**, **lumens**, **CRI**, and **SDCM** data
- A physical sample you can evaluate for finish, thermal behavior, and driver quality

Prototyping typically takes **2–3 weeks**. Expect one or two revision rounds before you approve.

### 3. Approval (确认)

You sign off on the prototype — appearance, photometric data, performance, and any sample test results. This is the point where the spec is **frozen**. After approval, changes get expensive because tooling and production parameters are locked in. Read the checklist in our [custom manufacturer buyer guide](/blog/how-to-choose-custom-track-lighting-manufacturer-2026/) so you know exactly what to verify before you say yes.

### 4. Mass production (量产)

Once approved, the factory runs the order. During mass production a real OEM factory follows **IQC → IPQC → OQC**:

- **IQC** — incoming quality control on components (LED bins, drivers, housings)
- **IPQC** — in-process quality control on the line
- **OQC** — outgoing quality control, including a **100% aging test** (burn-in) before packing

### 5. Quality control (质检)

Beyond the in-line QC above, finished goods should pass a final inspection: electrical safety test, lumen and CCT verification against the approved sample, and a **carton drop-test** on packaging. If you use a third-party inspection company, the factory should accommodate the visit or the pre-shipment inspection.

### 6. Shipping (发货)

The order is packed, labeled, and shipped per your terms — FOB, CIF, EXW, or DDP depending on your logistics setup. You receive the compliance documents (certificates naming your SKU, test reports) alongside the shipment so customs clearance is smooth.

## What You Can Customize on OEM Track Lights

This is where OEM LED track lighting earns its cost. A real OEM manufacturer customizes the **light itself**, not just the label. Here is the full menu:

### Housing color & material (外壳颜色/材质)

- **Colors** — any RAL/pantone: black, white, silver, matte, anodized, custom brand colors
- **Finishes** — powder coat, anodizing, electroplating, textured, sandblasted
- **Materials** — die-cast aluminum (standard), extruded aluminum, or custom profiles for architectural or magnetic track

### Color temperature — CCT (色温)

Choose from standard bins or ask for a custom CCT in between:

- 2700K (warm hospitality/residential), 3000K (retail/hotel), 3500K (mixed), 4000K (retail/commercial), 5000K–6500K (supermarket/daylight)
- Custom CCT binning and **SDCM ≤ 3** for color consistency across a project — critical for retail chains where fixtures sit side by side

### CRI (显色指数)

- Standard: CRI 80–90
- Premium: **CRI 90+, CRI 95+** with strong R9 (reds) — the baseline for retail, museum, and hospitality in 2026
- Optional **UV/IR filtering** for artwork and merchandise protection

Use the [color temperature guide](/tools/cct-guide) and [CRI guide](/tools/cri-guide) to dial in the exact spec before you ask for a quote.

### Beam angle (光束角)

- Narrow spot (15°–24°) for accent and display lighting
- Medium flood (30°–40°) for general retail
- Wide flood / wall-wash (45°–60° and asymmetric) for grazing and ambient
- Custom **reflector + lens** engineering with an IES file — this is the sign of a true OEM factory, not a re-labeler

### Wattage & electrical (功率)

- Typical range for track heads: 5W–50W per head
- Custom driver selection for **constant current (CC)** or **constant voltage (CV)** platforms
- Voltage options: 120V, 220–240V, 277V, or low-voltage (24V) magnetic track systems

### Dimming protocols (调光协议)

- **TRIAC** — standard for North American residential dimmers
- **0–10V** — commercial and architectural standard
- **DALI / DALI-2** — building-management and control-system integration
- **PWM / Zigbee / Casambi / Matter** — smart-home and wireless control for e-commerce brands

## Packaging & Branding Options (包装与品牌定制)

For a private label product, packaging is part of the product. A complete OEM packaging program includes:

- **Custom logo printing** on the housing (laser engraving, silk screen, pad printing, or brand-color finishing)
- **Retail-ready box** with your brand, EAN/UPC barcode, spec sticker, and inserts
- **Carton & outer packaging** — custom corrugated cartons, foam inserts, retail display-ready options
- **User manual & compliance labels** — multilingual manuals, wiring diagrams, certification marks for your market
- **MOQ on packaging** — usually lower than the product MOQ; itemize it separately so you can test a market with a small run before scaling

## Lead Times & Payment Terms (交期与付款方式)

### Realistic lead times

| Stage                   | Time                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Prototype               | 2–3 weeks (with IES file) |
| Tooling + certification | 3–6 weeks                 |
| Mass production + QC    | 3–5 weeks                 |
| **Full OEM cycle**      | **8–14 weeks**            |

A factory that quotes "samples in a few days, no problem" without an IES file or a dated production plan is telling you it does not engineer. For repeat orders after the first production run, lead times usually compress to **4–6 weeks** because tooling is already done.

### Common payment terms

- **30% deposit / 70% balance before shipment** — the industry standard for OEM/ODM
- **Deposit before tooling**, balance split across production milestones for large tooling projects
- **Letter of credit (L/C)** for large or long-standing accounts
- Payment via T/T (bank transfer) most common; PayPal and credit card often available for sample orders

## FAQ

**What is the difference between OEM and ODM for track lighting?**
OEM means the product is built to your exact specification and branded for you. ODM means you take one of the manufacturer's existing (catalog) designs, customize it, and brand it. OEM gives you more control but usually needs tooling; ODM is faster and cheaper to start.

**What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for OEM track lighting?**
It depends on the design. True bespoke OEM with new tooling often starts at 1,000+ units to absorb tooling cost; ODM modifications may start at 300–500; private-label on a standard product can be even lower. MOQ should be negotiated per design and itemized with tooling separately.

**How long does an OEM track light order take?**
A prototype takes 2–3 weeks with an IES file; tooling and certification add 3–6 weeks; mass production and QC run 3–5 weeks. A realistic full OEM cycle is 8–14 weeks, compressing to 4–6 weeks for repeat orders.

**What can I customize on a private label track light?**
Housing color and material, CCT (2700K–6500K), CRI (80/90/95+ with optional UV/IR filtering), beam angle (15°–60° and asymmetric, with custom optics), wattage, dimming protocol (TRIAC, 0–10V, DALI, wireless), plus your logo, packaging, and manual.

**What payment terms are typical for OEM lighting?**
The standard is 30% deposit and 70% balance before shipment (T/T). Large tooling projects may be split across milestones, and L/C is available for large accounts. Samples are usually paid upfront.

**How do I verify an OEM track lighting factory is not a trading desk?**
Ask for workshop photos (not showroom), an IES file with your prototype, certificates naming your exact SKU, itemized MOQ and tooling before deposit, and installed-project references.
